### 📍 Where I come from

I spent **four and a half years on the PwC Ghana-led receivership** that resolved the
370+ institutions closed in Ghana's 2017–2019 banking-sector clean-up. Claims
resolution, AML/KYC verification, fraud detection, and five branches with 100+ staff
reporting in.

I now build the analytics layer on top of that operational base. Not from a tutorial
dataset — from the sector I worked inside.

---

### 📊 Flagship work

> **[ghana-banking-collapse](https://github.com/SamuelAtokwamebaah/ghana-banking-collapse)** ·
> [read the findings ↗](https://samuelatokwamebaah.github.io/ghana-banking-collapse)

Every financial institution whose licence the Bank of Ghana revoked in the clean-up,
compiled from the regulator's own published notices, with a hand-verified analysis of
why the 23 savings and loans companies failed.

| | |
|---:|:---|
| **418** | institutions resolved, compiled from primary sources |
| **17 of 23** | savings and loans failures citing related-party lending |
| **927.5 days** | median time insolvent institutions kept their licences |
| **GHS 2.30bn** | in net-worth deficits, two thirds of it in five institutions |

Archived on Zenodo under a DOI, CC BY 4.0, reproducible end to end from the published
CSVs. **One finding has been withdrawn and the reasoning published** — corrections are
recorded in a changelog rather than made silently.
